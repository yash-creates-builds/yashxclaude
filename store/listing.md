## Full description (max 4000)
```
Stop counting cooker whistles in your head. Seeti listens to your pressure cooker, counts every whistle, and tells you when to turn off the gas.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your dish: Dal, Rice, Rajma, Chole, Aloo, Khichdi or your own.
2. Set how many whistles. Seeti remembers it for next time.
3. Tap Start and get on with your work.
4. Seeti says each count out loud: "Ek seeti", "Do seeti"…
5. On the last whistle, a loud alarm says "Gas band karo!"

MADE FOR EVERY KITCHEN
• Voice in Hindi, English and Gujarati
• Big numbers you can read from across the kitchen
• Missed a whistle? Tap +. Wrong sound? Tap − or Undo.
• Two cookers at once: Seeti asks which one whistled
• Safe-to-open timer: tells you when the pressure is out
• Learns your cooker's whistle for better counting
• History: cook the same dish again in one tap

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Nothing is recorded or uploaded
• Sound is checked on your phone and thrown away
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Works without internet

Seeti is a helper. Always check your cooker yourself before opening it.
```
