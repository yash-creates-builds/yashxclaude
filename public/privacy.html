Seeti Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: Seeti uses your microphone only to hear pressure-cooker whistles. Sound is checked on your phone and thrown away immediately. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What Seeti does

Seeti ("the app") listens to a pressure cooker, counts its whistles, and alerts you when to turn off the gas.

Microphone

  • The app uses the microphone only while you are counting whistles.
  • Sound is analysed live, on your device, a fraction of a second at a time, and then discarded.
  • No audio is recorded, stored, or uploaded.

Information stored on your phone

To remember your choices, the app saves these on your device only: dish whistle counts, settings (voice language, timer, sensitivity), your cooker's whistle profile (pitch and loudness numbers, not a recording), and your recent cooking history. You can remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Information we collect

None. Seeti has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and does not share data with anyone.

Internet use

If you turn on "Smart listening", the app downloads a free sound-recognition model (about 15 MB) once. This download sends no personal information. Everything else works offline.

Notifications

The app may show alarms on your lock screen (for example "Turn off the gas") if you allow notifications. These are created on your phone and are not sent from a server.

Children

Seeti is meant for adults cooking at home and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Safety

Seeti is a helper, not a safety device. Always check your cooker yourself before opening it.

Changes
