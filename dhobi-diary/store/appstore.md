## Description
```
Replace the paper dhobi diary with one simple app.

GIVE CLOTHES
• Tap + on Shirt, Kurta, Saree, Bedsheet and more. Big buttons, no typing.
• Iron, Wash + Iron or Dry clean for each item.
• See the total count and approx. cost right away.

RECEIVE CLOTHES
• ✓ Came, ✗ Not back, or "Some" with big number buttons.
• A running count, "Received 5 of 7", until everything is back.
• Partly back? Finish it later, even on a past date.

DHOBI CAME TODAY
• One big button marks the date, day and time of every visit.

MONTHLY BILL
• Totals by service, a month calendar, mark as paid.
• Share the bill on WhatsApp in one tap.

MADE FOR EVERY FAMILY
• English, हिंदी, ગુજરાતી
• Grandma Mode: only Give, Receive and Bill, extra large.
• More than one dhobi? No problem.
• Undo, Excel (CSV) export and backup file.

PRIVATE
• Works offline. No sign-up. No ads. Free.
```
