## Full description
```
Replace the paper dhobi diary with one simple app.

GIVE CLOTHES
• Tap + on Shirt, Kurta, Saree, Bedsheet… big buttons, no typing
• Iron, Wash + Iron or Dry clean for each item
• See the total count and approx. cost right away

RECEIVE CLOTHES
• One tap: "All received"
• Or count each item — the app shows "1 Kurta missing" in red

MONTHLY BILL
• Totals by service, month calendar, mark as paid
• Share the bill on WhatsApp in one tap

MADE FOR EVERY FAMILY
• English, हिंदी, ગુજરાતી
• Grandma Mode: only Give, Receive and Bill, extra large
• More than one dhobi? No problem
• Reminders on the days your dhobi comes
• Undo after saving, Excel (CSV) export, backup file

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline. No login. No ads. Free.
• Your data never leaves your phone.
```
