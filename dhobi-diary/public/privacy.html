Dhobi Diary — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Dhobi Diary works fully offline. It has no ads, no analytics and no passwords.

  • What is stored: the bundles of clothes you enter, dhobi visits (date and time), dhobi names, rates and your settings.
  • Where: on your phone, inside the app's private storage.
  • Family diary (optional): if you start or join a family diary, the diary entries and the first name you type are also stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so the phones in your household can sync. Only phones that joined with your household's code can read them. Each phone signs in anonymously; no email, phone number or password is collected. Leaving the family diary stops syncing.
  • Sharing: nothing else leaves your phone unless you tap "Share bill", "Send code", "Export CSV" or "Save backup file" and choose where to send it.
  • Children: the app is not directed at children.

Deleting the app deletes the data on that phone. To delete a family diary's online copy, every member leaves it, or write to us and we will delete it.
