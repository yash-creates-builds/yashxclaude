Dhobi Diary — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Dhobi Diary works fully offline. It has no login, no ads, no analytics and no servers.

  • What is stored: the bundles of clothes you enter, dhobi names, rates and your settings.
  • Where: only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. Android's own device backup may include it if you have that turned on.
  • Sharing: nothing leaves your phone unless you tap "Share bill", "Export CSV" or "Save backup file" and choose where to send it.
  • Notifications: reminders are created on your phone. No internet is used.
  • Children: the app does not collect any personal data from anyone.

Deleting the app deletes all its data.
